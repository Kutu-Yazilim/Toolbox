Transform Interaction Model

How should users rotate, scale, and transform shapes?

A

Figma-style Bounding Box

When a shape is selected, a bounding box appears with 8 resize handles (corners + edges). Hovering just outside corners shows a rotation cursor — drag to rotate. Hold Shift to constrain proportions or snap rotation to 15° increments. A small rotation angle tooltip appears during drag.

Industry standard. Users already know this model.

B

Dedicated Transform Handles

Separate visible handles: a circular rotation handle above the shape, corner triangles for scaling. More explicit but takes more screen space. Similar to PowerPoint or Google Slides.

More discoverable for new users, but less elegant.

C

Figma-style + Properties Panel Input

Bounding box handles for direct manipulation (same as A), plus precise numeric inputs in the Properties panel for X, Y, Width, Height, Rotation, and Opacity. Best of both worlds — quick drag for rough positioning, exact values for precision work.
