Gauge Design Direction

Inspired by speedtest.net — which style feels right for your brand?

A — Neon Arc

Gradient arc with glow effects, thin needle, dark background. Closest to speedtest.net's modern look. Clean, techy, premium feel.

B — Segmented Ring

Color-coded segments (red/amber/green/cyan) with no needle — just a bold center number. Cleaner, more minimal, focuses on the reading itself.

C — Thick Arc + Dot Pointer

Bold gradient arc with a glowing dot at the current position instead of a needle. Ultra-thin font for the number. Futuristic, smooth feel.
